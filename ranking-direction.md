# Slickyads.com GEO/AEO Ranking Direction

## Quick take on the site

**What's working:**
- Clear service taxonomy (6 distinct services) — good for entity-based topical structure AI models like.
- Concrete proof points ($209K revenue, +40% traffic, +30% conversion lift) — these are exactly the kind of citable, specific facts that LLMs pull into answers when asked "who's good at X in Dubai."
- Personal-operator positioning ("you work with me directly") is a strong differentiator answer engines can quote.

**What's missing for GEO/AEO specifically:**
- No FAQ content/schema anywhere in the crawled pages — this is the single biggest lever for AEO (AI Overviews, Perplexity, ChatGPT browsing all favor Q&A-structured content).
- No dedicated location-service landing pages (e.g., "Google Ads agency Dubai" vs "Meta Ads management Dubai") — right now one page tries to rank for everything.
- Meta title/description still reads "Asim Khan — Marketing Operations" rather than leading with the commercial service + location, which is what both classic SEO *and* AI answer engines weight heavily for local commercial queries.
- Blog wasn't crawlable/visible in content — that's your AEO engine long-term (answer-style posts get cited far more than service pages).
